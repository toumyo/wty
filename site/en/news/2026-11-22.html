US stocks slump Friday; S&P 500 enters correction

A sharp sell-off in AI shares sent the S&P 500 down 3.7% to 6,910.39 on Friday, the Nasdaq composite down 5.3% to 22,617.73 and the Dow down 1.9% to 48,147.33. The S&P 500 is now more than 10% below its October 6 record, a correction, and its 8.6% weekly loss was its worst in more than 18 months.

02

Anthropic lists on Nasdaq in record US IPO

AI company Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share and began trading on Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker ANT. It opened at $111.50 and closed at $97.17, about 17% above the offering price. Reported proceeds of about $81bn make it the largest US IPO on record, surpassing SpaceX, while the Nasdaq fell 2.2% that day.

Anthropic filing shows $104bn nine-month net loss

Anthropic's SEC filing disclosed a net loss of $104bn for the first nine months of 2026, alongside third-quarter revenue of $17bn versus $1.4bn a year earlier. A separate $26bn private placement at the same $83 price is slated for buyers including Google, Nvidia, AMD and Microsoft.

Nvidia CEO Huang resigns; CFO named interim CEO; SEC probe reported

Nvidia said on November 12 that Jensen Huang had resigned as CEO, president and board member, effective immediately, and named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO. The Wall Street Journal reported that the SEC is investigating Nvidia, possibly including whether its investor disclosures were misleading.

Nikkei 225 plunges 5% to 56,437 as AI stocks lead losses

The Nikkei 225 fell 5.06% on Friday to close at 56,437, its lowest level since April and down nearly 8% on the week. SoftBank Group fell 14.5%, Kioxia 11.7%, Advantest 10.3% and Tokyo Electron 7.1%, with AI-related shares under heavy pressure.

06

Japan's October CPI rises to 2.3%, boosting December BOJ hike bets

Japan's national consumer price index rose 2.3% year on year in October, up from 2.1% in September and the highest since November 2025. The data strengthened expectations of another Bank of Japan rate hike in December; the policy rate is 1.25%.

07

Yen nears 160 per dollar; Japan's currency chief warns on intervention

The dollar touched 159.94 yen on November 12, its strongest in over two months. Currency chief Atsushi Mimura said it was "too early to forget" this summer's interventions, which included a joint action with the US. Japan's import price index rose 24.8% year on year in October.

08

Markets all but price in December Fed hike; dollar near 19-month high

According to CME FedWatch, markets have almost fully priced in a 25bp Fed hike in December, at about 97.8%. New York Fed President Williams said another hike may be needed, and St. Louis Fed President Musalem said several may be required. The dollar index is near its highest since April 2025 and the 10-year Treasury yield remains above 5%.

09

US October producer prices up 6.5% year on year, fastest since late 2022

The US Labor Department's October producer price index rose 6.5% from a year earlier, the fastest pace since November 2022, though the monthly increase was slightly smaller than expected. Inflation is broadening beyond energy, and the 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 5.156% on November 13.

Gold falls below $3,800; silver and platinum also weaken

Spot gold fell to $3,757 an ounce on November 18, its lowest since September 2025, as a firm dollar and rising Fed hike expectations weighed. Gold is more than 30% below its January record near $5,600. On the same day spot silver fell 3.5% to $45.75, and platinum has lost about 30% so far this year.
