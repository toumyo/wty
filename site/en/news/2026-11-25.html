Dow closes above 50,000 for first time as tech slides

On Tuesday, Nov. 24, the Dow rose 0.9% to 50,018.70, its first close above 50,000. The S&P 500 fell 0.5% to 7,201.09 and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.5% to 23,733.54, while most sectors outside tech gained.

Japan's October CPI at 2.3%, December BoJ hike seen likely

Japan's national CPI rose 2.3% year on year in October, and core CPI (excluding fresh food) rose 2.2%, above the BoJ's 2% target for a second straight month. Gov. Ueda signaled further rate hikes, and a 25bp hike at the Dec. 18 meeting is reportedly about 85% priced in.

ECB holds at 2.5%; Lagarde to step down early

The ECB kept its deposit rate at 2.5% on Oct. 29, the same day Christine Lagarde said she would leave on Feb. 7, 2027. Eurozone inflation reached 3.8% in September, and French 10-year yields climbed above 5% for the first time since 2002.

12

US banks' unrealized securities losses jump 50% in Q3

Unrealized losses on securities held by US banks rose 50% in the third quarter to $486 billion, according to FDIC data, as interest rates surged. Total bank loans grew 7.5% from a year earlier, the fastest quarterly pace since Q1 2023, and the FDIC said capital and liquidity remained strong.

13

Microsoft declares "code red" after mid-November outage

Microsoft declared a top-priority "code red" after a roughly 24-hour outage on Nov. 12–13 knocked out Outlook, Teams and Copilot, according to internal memos reported by Reuters. The flaw was introduced in July and went unnoticed for about four months; the company aims to fix future problems within an hour.
