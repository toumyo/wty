BOJ's Ueda sees inflation near 2% but flags upside risks

Speaking at an IMF seminar in Washington on November 8, BOJ Governor Ueda said underlying inflation should stay around 2% but with upside risks. Energy-driven price increases are expected to accelerate into early next year, and he said wages are consistent with 2% inflation.

S&P 500 Q3 blended earnings growth at 31.4%

As of November 6, 88% of S&P 500 companies had reported Q3 results, with blended earnings growth of 31.4%, according to FactSet data cited in a week-ahead preview. The figure comes from a market preview article published November 9.
