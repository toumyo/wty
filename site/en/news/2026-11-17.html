01

All three major U.S. indexes fall as AI and chip stocks weigh

On Monday, November 16, the Dow fell 739.38 points, or 1.41%, to close at 51,608.17. The S&P 500 dropped 145.08 points, or 1.92%, to 7,416.59, and the Nasdaq Composite lost 626.51 points, or 2.49%, to 24,509.76, the steepest decline of the three. Reports cite renewed concern about AI investment, semiconductor companies and elevated technology valuations.

Last Friday on Wall Street: Dow edges up while Nasdaq and S&P 500 slip, chip stocks weaken

On Friday, November 13, the Dow rose 0.1%, or 50.46 points, to 52,347.55. The S&P 500 fell 0.4% to 7,561.67 and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 0.9% to 25,136.27. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 3.4%, with AMD down 8.8% and Broadcom down 4.8%. For the week, the Dow gained 2.1%, while the Nasdaq and S&P 500 fell 2.3% and 2.5%.

Nikkei 225 falls 2.42% and closes below 60,000

The Nikkei 225 closed at 59,662.02 on November 16, down 1,480.33 points, or 2.42%. It was the first close below 60,000 since November 9. Advantest (6857) fell 11.12% and was the largest drag on the index, with reports citing selling in AI- and semiconductor-linked stocks.

06

U.S. Treasury yields rise as traders add to Fed hike bets

On November 10, two- to five-year Treasury yields rose more than 10 basis points and the 10-year yield climbed 5 basis points to 4.94%. Reports cite hot inflation, with core CPI up 0.3% month on month and headline CPI up 0.4%, and an unexpected jump in wage growth. Traders added to bets on at least two Fed hikes by mid-2027, and Fed Governor Christopher Waller backed a hike. The Fed raised rates by a quarter point in September and held in October.

07

Anthropic sets IPO terms: $61 billion raise, $1.2 trillion target market cap

According to Renaissance Capital, Anthropic plans to offer about 825 million shares at $70 to $78 per share, raising roughly $61 billion. At the midpoint, the fully diluted market value is about $1.2 trillion. A concurrent $26 billion private placement includes AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA and TSMC. Revenue for the 12 months ended September 30, 2026 was $35.3 billion, and the stock is expected to trade this week on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.
