Dow closes higher after erasing intraday loss; S&P 500 and Nasdaq slip

On November 25 the Dow Jones Industrial Average gained 164.25 points (0.33%) after falling about 1,100 points intraday. The S&P 500 fell 0.25% and the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.62%. US markets were closed on November 26 for Thanksgiving and reopen for a shortened session on November 27.

AI selloff deepens as Nvidia falls 5.9% Tuesday and 3.91% Wednesday

AI-linked stocks extended their slide on November 25. Nvidia dropped 5.9% on Tuesday and 3.91% on Wednesday, while AMD and Micron also fell. The Nasdaq 100 briefly sat nearly 20% below its record close, as investors worried about tighter AI regulation after Democrats swept the midterm elections.

03

Trump administration proposes new tariffs on 14 trading partners; China faces up to 20%

Following a probe into excess industrial capacity, the Trump administration proposed new duties on a wide range of imports from 14 major trading partners, including China. China faces the steepest levy at 20%, which would stack on roughly 25% tariffs enacted during Trump's first term.

04

October PCE inflation at 3.8% y/y; core beats forecast; Q3 GDP preliminary 4.3%

US PCE prices rose 0.3% month-on-month and 3.8% year-on-year in October, in line with expectations. Core PCE rose 3.3% y/y, slightly above the 3.2% forecast. The preliminary third-quarter GDP reading showed 4.3% growth.

05

Markets price ~97% chance of December Fed hike; officials turn hawkish

CME FedWatch data show markets assign a 97.8% probability to a 25 bp hike at the December FOMC and about 63% to another increase in January. On November 17, New York Fed President John Williams said the central bank would likely need to raise rates again, and St. Louis Fed President Alberto Musalem said several more hikes may be needed.

06

Nvidia board will not reinstate Jensen Huang; CFO Colette Kress interim CEO

Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO on November 12. On November 17 the board said it does not intend to bring him back and is seeking a permanent CEO, with CFO Colette Kress serving as interim leader. Huang and general counsel Tim Teter are scheduled to answer lawmakers' questions behind closed doors on December 1 and 2.

07

Nvidia Q3 revenue near $116B; guidance of $130B for next quarter beats estimates

Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of about $116 billion, with data center revenue of $107.6 billion, and EPS of $2.70 versus $2.48 expected. It guided to $130 billion for the quarter through January, above Wall Street forecasts. Shares rose about 5% in after-hours trading.

US Commerce tightens rules on remote access to advanced AI chips via overseas data centers

On November 16 the US Commerce Department announced new rules to prevent remote access to advanced AI chips and servers, closing a loophole that may have let Chinese companies use advanced Nvidia chips through overseas data centers. The Justice Department said the same day it is criminally investigating Singapore-based Megaspeed.
