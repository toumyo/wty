03

Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO

On November 23, Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as President, CEO and director, effective immediately. The board said it had met with him and received input from senior US government officials, and concluded his reinstatement poses no national security risk. Following press reports that he had been the target of a foreign intelligence effort, an independent committee is investigating. Colette Kress had served as interim CEO.

Tokyo core CPI rises 2.9% in November, below forecasts

Core consumer prices in Tokyo's central wards rose 2.9% year on year in November, up from 2.6% in October but below the 3.0% forecast. It was the fastest pace since July 2025 and the third straight month above the BOJ's 2% target. The core-core measure excluding fresh food and energy rose 2.5%, also below forecasts.
