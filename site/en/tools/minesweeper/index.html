Minesweeper

Classic Minesweeper that opens and plays right away. Choose easy, medium or hard, with a timer and a mine counter on screen.

Open the tool →

Free · No sign-up · Runs in your browser · Interface language：Simplified Chinese · Built by：Taiyang Wang

Features

  • Easy, medium and hard levels
  • Timer and mine counter
  • One-click restart
  • Runs in the browser, no download

How to use

  1. Choose a difficulty
  2. Click a square to reveal it. A number shows how many mines touch that square
  3. Reveal every safe square to win

FAQ

What are the rules?

Each number tells you how many of the eight surrounding squares hold a mine. Use the numbers to avoid the mines and reveal every safe square.
