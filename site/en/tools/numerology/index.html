Numerology (Life Code)

Enter your birth date and full name (in pinyin or English letters) to get a full numerology report, based on David A. Phillips' The Complete Book of Numerology.

Open the tool →

Free · No sign-up · Runs in your browser · Interface language：Simplified Chinese · Built by：Taiyang Wang

Features

  • Life path number: talents and life challenges
  • Talent grid: how digits 1 to 9 fall, with connections and missing numbers
  • Pinnacles and challenges for each stage of life
  • Name numbers: expression, soul urge and personality
  • Yearly, monthly and daily readings
  • An overall reading with advice

How to use

  1. Enter your birth date
  2. Enter your full name in pinyin or English letters
  3. Generate the report and read each part

FAQ

How should I enter my name?

Type your full name in pinyin or English letters. The name numbers are calculated from the letters.

Is it scientific?

No. Numerology is a traditional way of reading numbers, best used for self-exploration and fun.
